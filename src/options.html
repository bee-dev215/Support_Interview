5

Next conversation

Choose what kind of call it is. You can also switch in the panel with the mode button at the top. The two modes are separate: a free conversation shares only your API keys, model and microphone with interviews.

Job description Company name (and website if you have it), role, stack and the full job description. Update it before each interview; it's saved with the interview for the feedback report. Then click Research company: it searches the web for the company (products, business model, recent news, tech stack, culture and values, competitors) and analyzes the job against your CV profile. Answers use it whenever they ask about the company or the role, and to pick your examples that fit it best.
Company research Company snapshot, recent news, the role decoded, your fit and gaps, ready answers to "What do you know about us?" and "Why us?", likely questions and questions to ask them. Read it before the interview and fix anything that's wrong; your edits are used as they are.
Your topic Everything about this conversation, in your own words: what it's about, your intention (the point of view you want them to agree with), any facts or reasons you have, and the questions you want to ask them. Click Analyze topic when you're done to work out your goal, arguments and likely objections. During the call, replies answer their questions honestly, then guide them step by step toward your view. Your CV and personal notes are not used here.

Preferences

How the live panel behaves and looks. Changes apply immediately, even during an interview.

Auto-answer
Draft an answer as soon as the interviewer asks something. Turn off to answer only on demand.
Proactive (take the lead)
Don't just react: when you've finished speaking and there's a pause, or the interviewer shares something without asking, suggest what to say next to steer toward your strengths. Answers also get a short Hook line. L in the panel asks for a move any time.
Answer length
Short is easiest to glance at mid-sentence; Detailed suits deep technical rounds.
Reading guide
Simple words and short sentences, with highlighted words to stress. Answers always come in short paragraphs with / where to pause; this makes them easier still for English beginners.
Nice
Now and then, add a short, natural compliment for the interviewer to suggested replies: their look, their voice, their energy, or a thank-you for a friendly chat. At most one per reply, never repeated.
Photo
Show the lines prepared from an uploaded photo of the interviewer. When off, they stay hidden in the panel until you turn it back on.
Appearance
System follows your computer's light or dark mode.
Keyboard shortcuts
Work anywhere in Chrome, including while the Meet tab has focus. Inside the panel, press ? for more.
