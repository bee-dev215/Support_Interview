Hear the call from your computer

For calls outside this browser: Zoom, Teams, Skype or WhatsApp apps, a softphone for your second number, or your mobile's calls through Phone Link.

  1. Click Share computer audio.
  2. In Chrome's window, open the Entire screen tab, pick your screen and turn on Also share system audio, then click Share.
  3. Keep this window open. You can minimize it.
